Settings

Pages and files are fetched through Jina Reader. Nothing is stored on this site. Public pages only.

    What it checks

    AI crawler access

    robots.txt rules for 19 AI and search crawlers, split into search, user-triggered and training bots; noindex, nosnippet and max-snippet, which control AI Overviews; data-nosnippet; and canonicals.

    Readable without JavaScript

    The page is fetched twice, once as a browser renders it and once as most AI crawlers see it, then compared: how much content, and whether the title, H1, description and structured data survive without JavaScript.

    Answer-ready structure

    Subheadings, question-style headings, an answer-first opening, paragraph and section length, lists and tables, a summary, and an FAQ: what makes passages easy to retrieve and quote.

    Evidence and authority

    Named authors, publish and update dates, statistics, outbound citations to sources, quotations, About and Contact links, and Organization or Person schema with sameAs links.

    Machine-readable signals

    JSON-LD validity and content types, llms.txt and llms-full.txt format, meta description, Open Graph, image alt text and language.

    Engine readiness

    A per-engine view for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Microsoft Copilot: ready, limited or blocked, and why.
