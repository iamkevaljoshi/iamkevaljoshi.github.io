Settings
Private, staging or logged-in pages

Open the page, copy the rendered HTML (DevTools → Elements → right-click <html> → Copy outerHTML), paste it here and put the page URL above so styles load.

Powered by axe-core (Deque, MPL 2.0). Pages are fetched through Jina Reader and tested in an isolated frame in your browser.

    Page preview

    The page as tested, with its scripts removed. Use "Show on page" on any issue to highlight the element.

    What it checks

    WCAG 2.2 rules (axe-core)

    About 100 rules: contrast, alt text, form labels, names and roles of controls, ARIA validity, language, page title, landmarks, lists and tables, keyboard access, zoom and the new 2.2 target-size criterion. Each issue is mapped to its success criterion and level.

    Expert review

    What rules can't judge: alt text that exists but says "image" or a file name, generic or ambiguous link text, links opening new tabs without warning, the heading outline, skip links and landmark labelling.

    Manual WCAG 2.2 checklist

    Keyboard and focus (including Focus Not Obscured), screen reader output, reflow and zoom, text spacing, motion, media, forms, and the new 2.2 criteria: Dragging Movements, Redundant Entry, Accessible Authentication and Consistent Help.

    How to use the results

    • Fix critical and serious issues first. They block people completely: unlabelled buttons, missing form labels, keyboard traps, text that can't be read.
    • Fix the component, not the page. On AEM, Edge Delivery Services or any component-based site, most issues repeat across pages because they live in one component or block. One fix there clears hundreds of pages.
    • Check both viewports. Mobile menus, hidden labels and touch-target sizes often fail only on small screens.
    • Export CSV for your backlog: one row per element, with selector, HTML, how to fix and the WCAG reference.
    • Then test by hand. Unplug the mouse, turn on a screen reader (NVDA on Windows, VoiceOver on Mac and iOS, TalkBack on Android) and work through the checklist.
